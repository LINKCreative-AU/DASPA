DASP tax calculator: what actually lands in your bank

A full superannuation refund calculator for every visa type, fund-held or ATO-held, with the legislated DASP tax rates applied properly, plus a balance estimator if you have no idea what your super is worth.

How much of my Australian super will I get back? After DASP tax, working holiday makers (417/462) keep 35% of their balance, and most other temporary visa holders (482, student 500) keep 65% of the taxed element, but only while the super is still with the fund. Once it transfers to the ATO as unclaimed money (about six months after you leave), the tax is 65% for everyone. The calculators below apply the current rates for you.

Worked examples

  • Backpacker, 417, $5,600 with one fund: 65% tax = $3,640 withheld → $1,960 paid, about $1,796 after our fee.
  • Nurse, 482, $20,000 with her fund: 35% tax = $7,000 withheld → $13,000 paid, about $12,836 after the fee. The same balance claimed after the ATO transfer: $7,000 paid. Waiting costs her $6,000.
  • Student, 500, $2,400 across three part-time-job funds: 35% tax = $840 → $1,560 paid, about $1,396 after the one flat fee (it covers all three funds).

Calculator questions

How much tax is taken out of a DASP?

65% for working holiday makers (417/462), 35% on the taxed element for other temporary visas, 45% on any untaxed element, and 65% on ATO-held unclaimed super regardless of visa. The calculator applies these automatically.

Why is the working holiday rate 65%?

Parliament set a special 65% DASP rate for working holiday makers from 1 July 2017. No agent can reduce it. Anyone implying otherwise should be avoided. What you control is timing, and making sure every account is found.

How accurate is this?

It applies the current legislated rates to the balance you enter, treated as a taxed element. Which is what nearly all employer-paid super is. Your fund calculates the exact components at payment time, so treat results as a close estimate, not a quote.

How do I find my real balance?

Your fund's app or last statement is the quickest way. Multiple jobs usually mean multiple funds, and money may already be at the ATO, finding every account in your name is part of every DASPA claim, included in the flat fee.

Does the calculator include your fee?

Yes, as a toggle: $149 + GST ($163.90), flat, refunded in full if we can't recover any super. Untick it to see the DIY numbers; the ATO's free system produces the same tax outcome.
