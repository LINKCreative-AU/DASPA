Can I get my superannuation back after leaving Australia? Yes — if you were on a temporary visa that has expired or been cancelled and you've departed, you can claim your full super balance as a Departing Australia Superannuation Payment (DASP). Tax is withheld at 65% for working holiday makers (subclass 417 and 462) or 35% on the taxed element for other temporary visas. Claims are typically paid within 28 days of a complete application reaching the ATO and your fund.

Don't wait — after ~6 months your super moves to the ATO and the tax gets worse

Once you've left and your visa has ended, your fund is required to hand your balance to the ATO as unclaimed super money — usually within about six months. It's still yours, but there's a catch:

65%tax on ATO-held super paid as a DASP — regardless of your visa type 35%what most non-working-holiday visa holders pay if they claim before the transfer ~6 monthsuntil funds must transfer your balance to the ATO

If you were on a student or skilled visa, claiming before the transfer nearly halves the tax. Even for working holiday makers, money at the ATO earns only CPI interest instead of fund returns.

Can I do this myself for free?

Yes. The ATO provides a free DASP online application system at ato.gov.au, and if your affairs are simple it works well. People hire us because we're a Registered Tax Agent who finds every account (including ATO-held money you may not know about), deals with funds that demand certified documents, fixes rejected applications, and answers on WhatsApp — for a flat $149 + GST, never a percentage. Full honest comparison here.

What if you can't find any super in my name?

Then you pay nothing: no super, no fee — the $149 + GST is refunded in full. We only keep the fee when we actually recover super for you. (And if your balance turns out to be so small the fee doesn't make sense, we'll tell you before lodging rather than take the job.)

How much tax will be taken out?

DASP tax rates are set by legislation, not by us: 65% if you held a working holiday visa (417/462), 35% on the taxed element for other temporary visas, and 65% on any super already transferred to the ATO as unclaimed money. The estimator above shows what typically lands in your bank.

How long until the money is in my account?

The ATO and funds typically process complete DASP applications within 28 days. Our form takes about 5 minutes, identity verification a couple more, and we usually lodge within one business day of your ID clearing.

I don't know my super fund. Or my TFN. Can you still help?

Yes — that's most of our clients. Your TFN is optional (it helps us find every account, including ATO-held money), and "I don't know my fund" is a standard option on our form. We search for every account held in your name as part of the flat fee.

Can the money go to my bank at home?

Yes. We collect either Australian account details or your international account with its SWIFT/BIC code. The fund or the ATO pays you directly — we never touch or hold your money.

What if my visa hasn't expired yet?

A DASP can only be paid once your visa has ended and you've left. If you've left for good, you can ask the Department of Home Affairs to cancel your visa rather than waiting it out — we can guide you through that as part of your claim.

Is my identity information safe?

Identity verification is powered by Didit, a dedicated identity platform — passport, selfie and biometric checks happen in their secure environment, not in our inbox. See our privacy policy for how data is handled.
