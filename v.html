With us.

  • A holistic and educational approach to finance. You are actively involved and educated about the world of finance and what it involves. Your loan isn't just approved, you are given the detail you need to know.
  • Your financial situation is front and centre. Time is taken upfront to ensure your application will accurately reflect your financial strengths, so that you’re putting your best foot forward.
  • Your needs are the priority, and we ensure that they are met by the loan. Your loan will look after your needs, not only now, but in the future also.
  • Brokers that act on your behalf throughout the financing process, arranging the loan with the lender for you. Understanding individual lenders’ policies and “appetites” is critical, so that your loan approval chances are maximised
  • You deal with one broker through the whole process. So, you get personalised service and have one point of contact for any questions and enquiries. This means that your questions get answered, there is no confusion, and your needs are met.

Without us.

  • A stressful and confusing experience, as you deal with multiple different points of contact with a lender. You aren’t aware of how to emphasise the strengths of your financial position, so your chances of getting your loan declined increase.
  • Cookie cutter approach means you get a stock standard loan, rather than one which is customised, tailored, and structured to meet your individual needs.
  • As the loan application goes through more hands the process can be slowed down dramatically. This could cause you to miss your financing window on the sales contract of your new home.

Come have a chat

The home loan process.

Join us for a coffee Collapse

Simply walk us through what you’re looking to achieve. We’ll take the time to explain the lending and finance landscape, and start to map out a plan for you. For many people, this will occur years before they’re actually planning on buying.

Narrow down the options Expand

We look at your financial circumstances and begin the process of narrowing down lenders to help you get a great outcome. We get all the information required and then talk you through how the process will pan out with the lenders that we have identified for you.  

Application Expand

We submit the loan application on your behalf, and tirelessly chase it up to make sure it’s approved in time for you.  

Contract preparation Expand

After approval, the banks solicitors will prepare the loan offer and contract ready for you to sign. There will also be a settlement agent to arrange cheques and fund your loan. We sit with you to explain the loan contract, what it involves, and how to set up your bank accounts for ease of use.  

Follow up and support Expand

We keep in touch with you after the loan has settled. We continually re-approach the lender, trying to get you a cheaper rate than you currently have. It’s not a “set and forget” process, it’s one of continually working on your behalf so you don’t pay any more than you have to.

There are a lot of people who will review your home loan application throughout the process. We do all the work, so you don’t have to, allowing you to focus on the rest of your life.
