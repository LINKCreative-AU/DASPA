What happens to your passport photo

It travels over an encrypted connection into private storage that only our team can open. It is not posted anywhere, it is not attached to an email, and it is used for one thing: confirming you are who you say you are, which we are required to do before lodging your DASP claim on your behalf.

We keep it only as long as we need it. Once your identity is confirmed the images are deleted, and all we keep is the record that the check was done. If the check is never completed, they are deleted within 30 days of the upload anyway.
