When we're paid

The fee is paid securely by card (Stripe) when you submit your claim — it's what commissions the registered-agent work: the searches, verification and lodgement. It is not deducted from your super; your fund or the ATO pays your DASP directly to you in full (less the legislated tax).

Is the fee tax deductible?

Fees paid to a registered tax agent for managing your tax affairs are generally deductible. If you lodge a final Australian tax return for your departure year, you can usually claim it there.

The only honest asterisk

DASP tax (65% working holiday / 35% taxed element for other temporary visas / 65% on ATO-held money) is withheld by your fund or the ATO under legislation. It isn't a fee, it doesn't go to us, and nobody can reduce it. And if your balance is so small the fee doesn't make sense, we'll tell you — the ATO's free system exists and we're upfront about it.

Common questions

Are there really no other charges?

None from us. Your own bank may charge an inbound international transfer fee or apply its exchange rate — that’s between you and your bank; we have no part in it.

What if you can’t find any super in my name?

Then you pay nothing — no super, no fee. We tell you what we found (or didn’t) and refund the $149 + GST in full. We don’t keep money for lodging nothing.

Is GST charged if I’m overseas?

The advertised price is $149 + GST ($163.90). Where a sale to a non-resident qualifies as GST-free, the checkout reflects the correct treatment at payment — the receipt you get is the source of truth.

Do you charge more for multiple funds?

No. One fee covers every fund plus ATO-held money. Multiple funds is the normal case, not an upsell.
