For the team. Two parts: **what's implemented** (and why), and **what to do next**.

### Technical implementation

- **Schema (JSON-LD)** on every page: FAQPage everywhere Q&As exist (visible text and schema kept
  in sync. Never let them drift); Organization + Service on the homepage; WebApplication +
  BreadcrumbList on the calculator; Article + BreadcrumbList on the guide.
- **Canonical tag** on every page; **Open Graph + locale** on every indexable page.
- **Sitemap.xml** lists all indexable pages; **robots.txt** allows everything except the claim flow.
- **Deliberately noindexed:** `/claim`, `/verify`, `/confirmation`, `/upload-form`, transactional steps with
  personal context; indexing them adds nothing and risks thin-content dilution. Everything else
  is indexable.
- **Social cards**: every indexable page has a branded 1200x630 `og:image` plus `twitter:card`,
  generated by `scripts/build-og.py`. Regenerate after changing a headline.
- **Self-hosted typeface**: Plus Jakarta Sans is served from `/assets/fonts` (two variable
  subsets, 49KB total), not from fonts.googleapis.com, so no page blocks render on a third party.
- **Crawlable calculator**: `/dasp-calculator` carries a pre-computed payout matrix and a
  wages-to-balance table built from the same maths as the JS tools, because a client-rendered
  calculator is invisible to Google. `scripts/build-calculator-matrix.py` asserts its output
  against the worked examples already on the page, so the two cannot drift.
- **Internal linking**: the footer is grouped by service and carries the two indexes. Country
  and visa pages had 1 to 2 inbound links each before that change; legal pages had 28.
- **Clean URLs** (Vercel), fast static pages, no JS frameworks, images pre-sized. Mobile-first CSS, the research shows ~half the clean volume is departed travellers on phones overseas.
- **Answer-first pattern** on every content page: bolded direct answer with current rates up top,
  "Source: ATO" attribution, last-updated date. This is written for featured snippets and AI
  overviews as much as for blue links.
- **Internal linking:** header carries "DASP Calculator" site-wide; footer on every page links the
  calculator, application page and guide; guide ↔ application ↔ calculator ↔ visa pages all
  cross-link. Some content overlap between pages is deliberate (each page must stand alone for
  its cluster); canonicals keep it clean.

### Trust/E-E-A-T signals (matters in this niche)

Registered Tax Agent number 26076969 linked to the live TPB register in header, footer and body
copy; ABN linked to ABN Lookup; official white TPB badge in the footer; "no super, no fee"
guarantee stated consistently; the honest "you can do this free at the ATO" answer kept in FAQ
contexts (per James's positioning call). Honesty is the differentiator against percentage agents.

**Ongoing habits**
4. **Refresh "last updated" honestly**, whenever rates/process are re-checked, update the date
   stamps (they're in the answer-first blocks). Stale dates erode snippet trust.
5. **Rank tracking**: watch these in Ahrefs/GSC, `dasp`, `dasp online application`,
   `dasp tax calculator`, `withdraw super leaving australia`, `claim superannuation leaving australia`,

6. **Reviews**: start capturing Google/Trustpilot reviews from paid-out clients (the email at

**Content roadmap (in priority order)**
7. **Language pages: built, noindex, and reprioritised by real data.** The Korea 700/mo
   and Taiwan 450/mo figures above are volumes for the ENGLISH term "dasp" typed by people
   in those countries. Ahrefs on the LOCALISED queries (21 Aug 2026) tells a different story:

   | Market | Native head term | Vol | KD |

   | Japan | スーパーアニュエーション 返金 | 150 | 0 |

   | Taiwan | 澳洲退休金申請 | 50 | - |

   So the order flipped. **Taiwan and Japan carry real native demand at KD 0; Korea does
   not.** Koreans search the English term, which the English site already serves, so Korean
   is ONE page kept for trust and conversion, not an SEO asset, and is not built out further.
   Taiwan has four pages and Japan four, each targeting its own head term (one page, one head
   term applies inside a language too). Generated by `scripts/build-language-pages.py`, which
   records the volumes next to the pages they justify.

   **All nine ship `noindex`.** A native reader signs off the copy AND validates the target
   queries before the tag comes off, one page at a time. They also need a visible language
   switcher, which is not built.

9. **Brazil is done, and it is an ENGLISH page, not a Portuguese one.** 250/mo on "dasp" at
   KD 0, ahead of the UK, but every Portuguese phrasing tested returns zero, so Brazilians
   search the English term like Koreans do. `/brazil` therefore joins the country set rather
   than the language set, and carries no translation risk. It is the one country page that
   leads with **35%, not 65%**: Home Affairs caps the Work and Holiday (462) arrangement with
   Brazil at 500 grants a year, so most Brazilians who worked here were on a student 500 or a
   skilled 482. That also makes the six-month ATO transfer cost them far more than it costs a
   European backpacker, because it takes them from 35% to 65% rather than 65% to 65%.
   Still unexplored: India 150/mo and Indonesia 90/mo, both with no page.
8. **Question posts** feeding the guide: "how much is super taxed when leaving Australia",
   "can I claim tax back on super withdrawal", "DASP processing time 2026", one page per
   question cluster, answer-first, interlinked.
9. **More nationality pages** as claim data shows demand (Italy, France, Spain all show volume).
10. **SERP check** (from the research's next-step note): confirm who ranks for

**Paid (when ready)**
11. Google Ads geo-targeting per the research: Australia + South Korea, Taiwan, UK, Japan,
    Ireland, Indonesia, Philippines, Thailand, Malaysia. `dasp tax` carries the cluster's top
    CPC ($6 USD). Commercial intent is proven.

**Rules to keep**
- The ATO-free-option honesty lives in FAQ contexts and the DIY page only, not in hero/selling
  sections (James's call, 9 July 2026).
- Never state a DASP tax rate without the ATO source line next to it.
- Every new page: answer-first block + FAQPage schema + canonical + OG + sitemap entry, copy the pattern from /dasp-online-application.
- **A meta description must not answer the question.** Title 60 characters, description 158.
  Name the question and promise what the SERP cannot show, then stop. At position 6 to 9 a
  complete answer in the description gets read and skipped, which is a ranking that earns no
  click. The figures belong on the page, where they were always the point.
- **No em dashes** anywhere in the copy. Comma, colon, full stop or middot.
- **Run `python scripts/check.py` before pushing.** It enforces every rule above and fails the
  GitHub check if you do not. It also documents, in its own docstring, what it cannot see.
